# Novex client intake checklist

Send this to the client before the build. Everything marked "must have" is needed before the site goes live. The rest can follow.

## Business basics (must have)
- Business name exactly as it should appear, and the trading name if different
- Street address, suburb, postcode
- Phone number and the email enquiries should go to
- Opening hours, including public holidays
- ABN (needed to register a .com.au domain)

## Domain and email (must have)
- Do they already own a domain? Which company is it registered with, and who has the login?
- Do they use email on that domain (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, other)? These records must be kept when we change DNS.
- If they don't own one yet: their preferred domain, registered in the business's name, not Novex's

## Photos (must have)
- At least 4 strong landscape photos for the hero: the room, the product, a signature item, a detail shot
- 2 to 3 more for the menu or services and the "about" section
- Their logo as an SVG or a large transparent PNG
- Shoot or supply them at full size. No screenshots, no photos of photos, nothing with other businesses' branding

If they have no good photos, quote a half-day shoot or agree on generated demo imagery as a temporary stand-in.

## Content
- Menu or services list with prices (a PDF or photo of the current menu is fine)
- Two or three sentences in the owner's words: who they are, why they started, what they care about
- Anything seasonal or regular worth listing (events, specials, trading changes)
- Function or catering details, if they offer them: capacity, minimums, how to enquire

## Bookings, forms and links
- Booking system and its public booking link (Now Book It, OpenTable, SevenRooms, other), if they use one
- Where enquiry form emails should go
- Instagram, Facebook and any other profile links
- Google Business Profile: do they have one, and can Novex get manager access?

## Rules we never break
- Reviews on the site are the client's real Google reviews only, quoted word for word
- No invented awards, stats or claims
- The client owns their domain and their content
